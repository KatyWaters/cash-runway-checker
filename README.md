# Cash Runway Checker

Cash Runway Checker helps small business owners see how their cash balance may change over the next six months. Enter your opening cash balance, monthly costs, minimum cash buffer, and expected client invoice payments to view a month-by-month forecast in pounds. Months with a negative closing balance are highlighted in red; positive balances below the buffer are highlighted in amber. The forecast summarizes the lowest balance and says whether it falls below the buffer.

## How to use

1. Enter your opening cash balance, monthly costs, and minimum cash buffer (default £1,500).
2. Add each expected client invoice and choose the month you expect it to be paid.
3. Review the forecast and adjust your inputs to explore different scenarios.
