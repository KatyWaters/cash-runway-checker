# Cash Runway Checker

Cash Runway Checker helps small business owners see how their cash balance may change over the next six months. Enter your opening cash balance, monthly costs, and expected client invoice payments to view a month-by-month forecast in pounds. Months with a negative closing balance are highlighted, and the forecast summarizes the lowest balance.

## How to use

1. Enter your opening cash balance and monthly costs.
2. Add each expected client invoice and choose the month you expect it to be paid.
3. Review the forecast and adjust your inputs to explore different scenarios.
